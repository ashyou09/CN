3. VIVA EXPLANATION GUIDE FOR PROFESSORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Why can't we read HTTP headers directly inside the Wireshark packet capture?
A: Because TLS encryption terminates at Layer 7. Wireshark inspects packets on the
   wire between Client and Edge, where the payload is AES-GCM encrypted Application Data.
   To see plaintext headers, we inspect them via curl -v or browser Developer Tools.

Q: What are Sequence and Acknowledgement numbers, and why are they critical?
A: Sequence numbers track the exact byte count sent over the TCP connection, ensuring
   reassembly in correct order despite network packet reordering. Acknowledgement (ACK)
   numbers tell the sender the next byte number the receiver expects, confirming delivery
   and triggering TCP retransmission if packets are dropped.
